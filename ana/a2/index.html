My Recipe- Butter Pecan Cake

By: Ana Karina Soloviov

This recipe shows you how to make a delicious, soft, and buttery pecan cake using simple inredients that can be found in any household. This butter pecan cake is extremely easy to make on a tight schedule and a budget since its made with Betty Crocker Cake Mix. It serves around six people in one sitting. This recipe will also include how to make vegan frosting that can be served with the cake.

What you'll need:

  • Betty Crocker Butter Pecan Cake Mix
  • 1 cup Milk of Choice
  • ½ Cup Vegetable Oil
  • 3 Eggs
  • ½ Coconut Oil
  • 1 Cup Powdered Sugar

Instructions:

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 350°F. Grease the pan with butter and lightly sprinkle flour over it, to avoid the batter sticking to the pan.
  2. Mix the Betty Crocker Cake Mix, milk of choice, vegetable oil and eggs in large bowl with mixer on medium speed or beat vigorously by hand 2 minutes. Pour the mixture into the greased pan.
  3. Bake for 40 minutes or until toothpick inserted comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes before removing from pan. Cool completely before frosting.
  4. While cake is baking, pour ½ of Coconut Oil and 1 Cup of Powdered Sugar into bowl and mix with mixer on medium speed until creamy, fluffy and white.
  5. Pour frosting on top of cake and enjoy!
