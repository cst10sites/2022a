PAIGES MINSET AND PASSION FOR BASKETBALL

Paige had to develop a strong mindset and passion for basketball, to be where she is now. To be a top of the top player like Paige, your mindset has to be sharp and positive 99% of the time. Paige says in an interview how since the time that she was a kid, she adored playing basketball. It was her favorite thing in the world to bounce and shoot for as long as she could remember. She made it clear that she never felt any sort of pressure to play the sport, since she genuinely loved it and was only doing it because she loved to do it. She also stated that she never thought of herself as being 'really good' just that she was able to do the basic moves uncommonly well, which put her at the top. This shows that Paige truly has a strong, unbreakable mindset which helped her tremendously to be the top player on multiple teams. Unfortunately, recently this year Paige has suffered a knee fracture, meaning that it'll take her at least 6 weeks to heal. This was a huge road block in her career since she had a commitment with the Uconn basketball team. With all this pressure on her shoulders, Paige is still keeping her head high, going to the gym everyday to practice shooting and ball handling while sitting down, showing her commitment and love for the sport that she plays.
