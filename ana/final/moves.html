PAIGES SIGNATURE MOVES

Paige Bueckers is a shifty and agile 5'9 pointguard for the Uconn basketball team. As one of the shorter players, Paige had to learn how to use her speed and agility to benefit herself and her teammates in game situations. Meaning that she had to become great at pulling up when shooting, reading the defense at the top of the key and passing the ball to the post players on her team. Paige is great at evolving in whichever game environment that she's put in. If there are bigger, taller defenders on the other team, she learns to adapt by staying low when handling the ball, and making great space between her and her defender by performing wide crossovers and quick shots. Her signature moves include her iconic hesitation then pull-up shot, her wide open three point shots, and finally, her remarkable floaters.
