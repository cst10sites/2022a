My recipe

Why I chose an army sandwich and black tea:

I will be explaining how to make what I like to call an army sandwich and tea. An army sandwich is an open sandwich which consists of a slice of bread with butter, ham, slices of tomato(es) peppered with some black pepper. This dish is the creation of my father because he had to serve for two years in the Polish military because it was mandatory. He made closed sandwiches with the same ingredients but decided to make it an open sandwich becuase the tomato was making everything soggy. I like to eat it because it simple, easy to make, fills you up and is savory. I also will be explaining how to make black tea. This recipe is from my grandfather who would always make it for me to cheer me up. I love it because it is warm and has an appealing aroma.

Ingredients for making an army sandwich:

  • One avg. sized tomato
  • One slice of bread of your choosing
  • Black pepper
  • Romaine lettuce leaves
  • Bell peppers(of all colors)
  • Butter(a butterknife's worth)
  • Two slices of ham
  • 3½ cups of water
  • One teabag of black tea
  • 2tbsp. of sugar
  • Toaster

Instructions:

Steps to making an army sandwich and a cup of tea:

  1. Take your pieces of bread and toast them in a toaster.
  2. Cut your tomato up into appropriately sized slices.
  3. Take your letuce plants and peel off the leaves
  4. Once your bread is toasted with a golden brown, take it out and put it on a plate.
  5. Take a butter knife and butter the piece of bread that you have.
  6. Put two slices of ham onto your bread.
  7. Put your romaine letuce leaves onto your bread.
  8. Put your slices of tomato onto your sandwich.
  9. Sprinkle some blake pepper onto your sandwich to give it some flavor
  10. Fill a pot with a minimum of 3½ cups of water and boil at 120°C
  11. Once it begins to bubble you can put your teabag into the boiling water.
  12. Once the tea becomes a strong, dark black color, pour it into your cup.
  13. Put 2tbsp. of sugar into your cup of tea
  14. Enjoy!
