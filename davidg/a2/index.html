How to make macaroni cheese

Today you will learn how to make macaroni cheese.

I like macaroni because its simple to make and is still quite delicious.

Ingredients:

  • 200g bow tie pasta
  • ¼ cup butter
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheese

Instructions:

  1. Heat a pot of lightly salted water at 100°C. Cook bow tie pasta in the boiling water, stiring ocationaly, for 8 minutes. Then drain.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat; stir in flour, salt, and pepper until smooth, about 5 minutes. Slowly pour milk into butter-flour mixture while continuously stirring until mixture is smooth and bubbling, about another 5 minutes. Add cheese to milky mixture and stir until cheese is melted, 2 to 4 minutes.
  3. Fold pasta into cheese until coated.
  4. Enjoy!
