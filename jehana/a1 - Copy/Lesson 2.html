Sugar Free Meringue Cookies

I'm a big fan of the texture, but in my household, the love for overly sweet munchies is very rare. Sugar free meringue cookies was one of the many compramises I made with my family allowing us to enjoy a nice snack. Not only is it a better alternative, they're also really fun to make and can turn into one of our bonding moments.

Ingredients

  • 2 Large Egg Whites (room temp - not from carton)
  • ¼ Sugar Free Powdered Sugar (Keto Powdered Sugar)
  • ½ Cream of Tartar
  • 1 Teaspoon Vanilla Extract (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 110°C/220°F
  2. Beat egg white until frothy
  3. Add cream of tartar and vanilla and beat until soft peaks form
  4. Add powdered sugar and beat until stiff peaks form
  5. Transfer mixture into piping bag with a star point
  6. Pipe out tablespoon portions onto prepared baking sheet
  7. Bake for 45mins
  8. Turn oven off and slightly open door (leave meringue inside for an hour)
  9. After an hour, remove then leave in room temp until cool

Tips

  • Use a patterned tip for decorative meringues
  • Make sure the bowl is completely dry. The slightest drop of liquid can alter the texture
  • Be careful when separating yolk and whites
  • Gradually add the sugar so it doesn't deflate
