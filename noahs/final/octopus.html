More information about the Blue-Ringed Octopus

Habitat and Location

The blue-ringed octopus can be found from Japan down to the waters of Australia and across from the Phillipines to Vanuatu.

They have TWO types of venom which are lethal enough to kill 26 adults. While you don't actually feel the bite, you feel the effects of the venom after a few minutes.

If you survive, your body begins to function again after 15 hours.

Victims that have survived 24 hours will feel normal again.
