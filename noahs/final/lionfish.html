More information about the Lion Fish

Habitat and Location

The lion fish can be found in the coral reefs of the South Pacific and Indian Oceans.

The lion fish use their fan pectoral fins to corner their prey. The spine of the lion fish can deliver a venomous sting.

Female lion fish can lay approximately 2 million eggs per year.

Lion fish are nocturnal.
