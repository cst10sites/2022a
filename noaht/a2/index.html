Englands lovely Shepherd's pie

Shepherd's pie,a common and inexpensive British dish originating from the sheep country in Scotland and northern England, Shepherds pie ussually consists of with a layer of cooked meat and vegetables, topped with mashed potatoes,and baked in the oven until the mashed potatoes are lightly brown. This food is so yummy because of when you taste all the mixes of favor from the meat, vegetables and mashed potatoes makes your taste buds explode.

Special Ingredients

  • 3 large potatoes peeled and quartered
  • 8 tablespoons ½ butter
  • 1 medium onion, chopped ½ cups
  • 1-2 cups vegetables diced carrots, corn, peas
  • ½ pounds ground round beef
  • ½ cup beef broth
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt, pepper, other seasonings of choice

How to make Shepherds pie

  1. Boil the potatoes Place the peeled and quartered potatoes in medium sized potCover with at least an inch of cold water. Add a teaspoon of salt. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook for 20 minutes

  2. Make some vegetables carrots, peas, corn etc cook them for a couple minuts until well browned

  3. After a little bit place some ground beef until its pink is all gone cook all together Cook uncovered for 10 minutes, adding more beef broth if necessary to keep the meat from drying out.

  4. mash the cooked potatoes When the potatoes are done cooking (a fork can easily pierce), remove them from the pot and place them in a bowl with the remaining 4 Tbsp of butter. Mash with a fork or potato masher, and season with salt and pepper to taste.

  5. now put the meat and all the vegetables and mash potatoes in a dish and Preheat oven to 400°C

  6. Place in a 400°C oven and cook until browned and bubbling, about 30
