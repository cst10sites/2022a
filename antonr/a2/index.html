Mac and Cheese Recepie (kraft)

I really like mac and cheese because it is very good, and I usually make it when Im alone and dont want to cook. I make this food because im hungry. Mac and cheese is macaroni and cheese, it has dairy. Mac and cheese is an easy food to make when you're really hungry because it takes about 10 minutes, more or less. To make the food you would need: running water, a stove( a fire would work too i guess), and a pot.

MATERIALS:

  • 6 cups of water
  • ½ table spoon of unsalted butter
  • 3 table spoons of milk

RECEPIE:

  1. Boil water at 100°c
  2. Stir macaroni in it for about 7 to 8 minutes
  3. Drain the macaroni
  4. Add the milk, butter, and cheese mix
