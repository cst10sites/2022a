What are M&M's?

M&M's are candies made in the United States, they are one the most
popular candies in the world, sold in almost every country. The candies where first made in New Jersey in 1941, made by
Forrest E. Mars Sr.

History

Eminems where invented in 1941. in the 1950's the eminems gained their famous "m" stamp
in 1954 they introduced peanut candies to their already normal m&ms. To identify the peanut m&ms from the regular ones, they
changed the stamp to a white color. M&Ms launched startbursed in the 1960s. In the 1980's M&M's became the first candy in space!
Still in the 80's, M&Ms finally become a global brand, selling in Oceania.
In the 90's M&M's launched milk chocolate candies. M&Ms are branded as the "Candy of the Millenium" in the 2000's.

Products

M&MSs have 5 different main flavors. Chocolate almond, mint chocolate, mocha, resberry almond and tripple chocolate(milk, dark, and white chocolate.)
In additon to the regular beads, they sell multiple types of chocolate bars: peanut bar, normal chocolate bar, crisp rice bar, almond bar, and mint bar.
They also sell many types of childeren toys, and ice cream m&m flavors.
Some stores like SafeWay and Subway sell m&m cookies!
