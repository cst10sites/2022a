This recipe is a pretty good mac & cheese recipe I found off of the internet. I chose it because it has a cheese crust on top, which I love. It also makes foue servings, so you get to eat it four times!

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces uncooked elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 3 cups milk
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 2 ½ tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • ½ bread crumbs
  • 1 pinhc paprika

Instructions

  1. Cook macaroni according to package directions, then drain.
  2. In a saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Stir in enough flour to make a roux. Add milk to roux slowly, stirring constantly. Stir in cheese, and cook over low heat until cheese is melted and the sauce is a little thick. Put macaroni in large caserole dish, and pour sauce over macaroni. Stir well.
  3. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add breadcrumbs and brown. Spread over the macaroni and cheese to cover. Sprinkle with a little paprika.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 minutes. Serve.
