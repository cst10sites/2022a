Aglio E Olio

Today we will be making the world renound Aglio E Oglio pasta. Aglio E Oglio is a delicious dish with many many flavours that will blow your mind. This recipe is so popular because of it's simplicity.

Ingredients

  • Pasta of your choice
  • ⅓ cup of olive oil
  • 8 Garlic cloves
  • 1 cup of grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup minced parsley
  • ½ teaspoon of crushed red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. Start by filling a pot with water ( make sure it's big enough for your pasta). Salt the water and wait for it to hit 100°C. Once it hits 100°C add your pasta and cook for recommended time on package. Once done drain the pasta and save 1½ cups of the pasta water.
  2. Meanwhile, heat up the olive oil on medium heat in a pan large enough to fit the pasta. Add the garlic and stir and cook for 2 minutes or until the garlic looks golden brown. Add in the red pepper flakes and cook for approximately 30 seconds more. Add the pasta water andadd a teaspoon of salt. Let cook until the liquid has reduced by ⅓.
  3. Add in the pasta and mix it in with the garlic sauce. Mix well then add the parsley and parmesan and mix. Let the pasta rest. Serve with a side of parmesan.
