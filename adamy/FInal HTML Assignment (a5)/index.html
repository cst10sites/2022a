Brad Marchand

Brad Marchand (born in 1988) is a 33 year old hockey player from Halifax currently playing for the Boston Bruins. Known for being a dirty player, Marchand has the nickname "The Rat". Drafted 71st overall by the bruins he became a starter on their roster in 2010-2011, winning the Stanley Cup against the Vancouver Canucks. Currently he is Boston's top player playing alongside David Pastrnak and Patrice Bergeron. He is also part of Team Canada's World Cup of Hockey Team winning Gold in 2016.
