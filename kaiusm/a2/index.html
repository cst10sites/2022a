-   Easy Pancake Recipe   -

Today we will be making some easy to make, homemade pancakes. With only five easy steps, this recipe is sure to satisfy. Make that perfect breakfast for you or the whole family!

Level: Beginner Prep Time: 10 min. Yields: 4 servings
Total Time: 20 min. Cooking Time: 12 min.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups of all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp. of sugar
  • 1 tbsp. of baking powder
  • ¼ tsp. of salt
  • ⅛ tsp. of ground nutmeg
  • 2 large eggs *
  • 1¼ cups of milk *
  • ½ tsp. of pure vanilla extract

* at room temperature

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and nutmeg, in a large bowl.
  2. Beat the eggs, in another bowl and then whisk in the milk and vanilla.
  3. Over medium heat, melt the butter in a large cast iron skillet.
  4. Whisk the butter into the milk mixture. Add the wet ingredients to the flour mixture, and whisk until a thick batter is formed.
  5. Keep the skillet at medium heat and ladle about ¼ cup of the batter onto the skillet. Ladle about 1 or 2 more ¼ cups of batter on the sillet. Cook until bubbles begin to form on the pancake's surface, and the bottoms are golden brown. This usually happens after about 2 minutes. Flip with a spatula and cook about 1 minute more on the other side. Repeat with the rest of the batter and add butter to the sillet as needed.
