Popcorn recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons coconut oil or extra virgin olive oil
  • ⅓ cup popcorn kernels
  • 1 tablespoon butter or more to taste, optional
  • Salt(to taste)

Method

  1. Heat the Oil:

      -Heat your oil in a saucepan at 165 °C or medium heat.

  2. Put 2 Popcorn Kernels Into the Hot Oil:

      -Wait for all of the kernels pop.

  3. Add All the Kernels:

      -Once the kernels pop add the remainder of your kernels into the saucepan and level out all the kernels.

  4. Cover Pan and Remove From Stove:

      -Count to 30 so that your kernels are nearly popping and this way when you put them back on the stove they will all pop.

  5. Return Pan to Stove:

      -The popcorn will begin popping soon. Once the popping starts, gently shake the pan by moving it back and forth over the burner. Tip: As the popcorn pops, try to keep the lid   slightly open to let the steam from the popcorn release.

  6. Once the Popping Slows to Several Seconds Between Pops Remove the Pan From the Stove:

      -Remove the lid, and dump the popcorn immediately into a wide bowl.

  7. Melting the Butter:

      -Use your hot saucepan to easily melt the butter. Put your butter in the popcorn and distribute it around the bowl. Tip: Let your butter brown a little to add a more intense buttery   taste to your popcorn.

  8. Sprinkle with Salt and Taste:

      - This recipe is finished, but add more topping if you would like.
