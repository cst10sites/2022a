How often should I replace my pillows?

You should replace your pillows around every 2 years.

Here are some signs you should replace your pillow:

  1. It smells bad
  2. Noticeable lumps
  3. You wake up with shoulder or neck pain
  4. You wake up sneezing
  5. You wake up tired
