A pillow is a rectangluar cloth bag stuffed with feathers, foam, rubber, or other soft materials, used to support the head when laying down. Pillows can be used for decoration or comfort.

Pillows have definite lifespans. Depending on the type of material, the pillows lifespan varies. Feather and down pillows could last up to 5-10 years. Polyester pillows should be replaced after 2 years
