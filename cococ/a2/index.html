Introduction

The food we will be making is chocolate chip cookies! I chose to make this because I love chocolate chip cookies and I wanted to share this recipe.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 and ¼ cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. In a large mixing bowl beat the butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar on medium speed until combined.
  2. Beat in the egg and vanilla.
  3. In a separate bowl combine the flour, cornstarch, baking soda and salt. Add into the wet ingredients, then beat on low speed until combined.
  4. On low, beat in the chocolate chips.
  5. Cover and chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour and up to 2 days.
  6. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
  7. Bake for 11 to 12 minutes or until golden brown around the edges.
