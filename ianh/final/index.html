MARIO KART WII

Mario Kart Wii is the 8th installment into the Mario kart series made by Nintendo. Mario Kart Wii is the game that I grew up with and is probably my most played game on the Wii. MarioKart Wii is the best racing game ever made (not biased) not for it having the best tracks or characters. I believe it is the best because of the physics engine and the ultra shortcuts. The physics engine along with the ultra shortcuts allows for insane races being as short as 16 seconds. also, the game is a pretty big modded community which the most popular being CTGP. CTGP allows you to play online amongst others (Nintendo stoped online play on May 20, 2014) while also adding 216 tracks and new game modes. Back to the physics of this game: the game engine allows you to do crazy things like clip through walls and intentionally hit walls to give you momentum upward to create a shortcut. These are only some of the reasons I enjoy Mario kart Wii and believe it is one of the best racing games.
