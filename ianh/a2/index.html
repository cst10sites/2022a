Introduction

The recipe I decided to make is Chocolate Chip Cookies. I chose this beacuse who does not like chocolate chip cookies. Also they are pretty straight forward to make
while not being to time consuming to make either. Also fun fact Chocolate Cookies were invented in 1938 by a american chef who decided to put a Nestlé chocolate bar
in a cookie which supprised me because I thought the cookies would have been around much longer

Ingredients

  • ¼ of 4 cups of soften butter
  • ¼ of 4 cups of brown sugar
  • ¼ of 4 cups of white sugar
  • ⅙ of a dozen eggs (2)
  • 384 grams of all purpose flour
  • ½ a teaspoon of salt
  • ¼ of 8 cups of semi sweet chocholate chips

Instructions

  1. preheat oven to 350° F
  2. mix white and brown sugar into butter in a bowl
  3. throw eggs into bowl and mix
  4. stir in flour and chocolate chips with you hand (for better flavor)
  5. add salt
  6. throw cookies on pan and then in oven for 10 minutes or until edges golden brown
