Mac and Cheese

Mac and cheese is a simple and comforting dish anybody can make no matter the skill level

Ingredients

  • your choice of pasta noodles(8 ounce)
  • ¼ cups of butter
  • ¼ cups of flour
  • ½ cups of salt
  • ground pepper to taste
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups of shreaded cheddar cheese

Directions

  1. step 1:

    Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil (212°F). Cook the pasta in the boiling water, stirring occasionally until cooked through but firm to the bite, 8 minutes. Drain.

  2. step 2:

    Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat; stir in flour, salt, and pepper until smooth, about 5 minutes. Slowly pour milk into butter-flour mixture while continuously stirring until mixture is smooth and bubbling, about 5 minutes. Add Cheddar cheese to milk mixture and stir until cheese is melted, 2 to 4 minutes.

  3. step 3:

    Fold macaroni into cheese sauce until coated. and serve!

Tips

You can never have enough cheese, and I usually add an extra half cup without ruining the recipe. Keep a careful eye on the milk when boiling, as milk boils fast and burns easily. Burnt milk will show up in the recipes flavor if overcooked.
