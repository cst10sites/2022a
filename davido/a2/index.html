scrambled eggs

ETC: 5-10 min

one of my favorite food is scrabled eggs. this is because it tastes good, and can be used in combonation with so many other foods.

ingrediants

  • ½ of 4 egg
  • a pinch of salt
  • 500ml of pepper
  • any thing else you want to add in

instructions

  1. First take your 2 eggs and crack them into a bowl. whisk for a minute
  2. Get a pan and put it on a stove. pour two tsp of olive oil into your pan
  3. After whisking, put a pinch of salt and DUMP 500 mls of pepper into the bowl for that little bit of extra spice
  4. At this point you can add anything you want to the bowl as eggs are viable with so many things. I would suggest greens though
  5. Then, heat up the pan with the oil in it turn it up to 65°c. try to get the oil all over the pan
  6. Wait for about 15 seconds then pour all contents from the bowl into the pan.
  7. Once you get all contents out of the bowl, get a wooden spoon, and stir the eggs every few seconds. You can tell the eggs are done once they are significantly less runny then how they were before.
  8. once your done, you put your eggs onto a plate and voila! the eggs are ready to serve
