ARIANA GRANDE'S PERFUME LINE

Ariana Grande had been creating perfumes and fragrances since 2015. She has all together 11 fragrances and she will probably come out with more in the future because her perfumes are all very successful.

PERFUME DESCRPTION YEAR
Ari Ari is a very sweet fruity fragrance. It smells like sweet grapefruit and vanilla and it's also a little bit powdery, with many other notes. It was her first ever perfume and it's one of the most popular and also one of my personal favourites. It has very good reviews. The only thing about a lot of Ariana Grande fragrances, is that they often don't have good lasting power. 2015
Frankie Frankie is a sweet, woody, powdery and fruity perfume. It's supposed to smell like apricot, pink pepper, pear, sugar and some other base notes. It was named after her half brother Frankie. This perfume has pretty good reviews, it's labeled as a unisex perfume, although it is very sweet. 2016 (limited edition)
Sweet like candy Sweet like candy is a very sweet fragrance. It's sweet and fruity with a lot of vanilla. It smells mostly like blackberry pear (pear is very common in her perfumes) bergamot, marshmallow and whipped cream. Many people say that SLC and Ari smell similar and that SLC is a creamier version because of the whipped cream. But in my opinion they smell different, mostly because of the grapefruit in ari. Sweet like candy is also a very popular perdume wiht good reviews, mostly the only complaint is the scent being overwhelmingly sweet. 2016
Sweet like candy Limeted edition SLC limited edition is basically a fruitier version of the original SLC. Marshmallow and whipped cream base notes rather than mid for the limited edition. 2017
Moonlight Moonlight is fruity sweet and powdery. It smells like plum and blackberry, marshmallow and pear. It is more powdery than her other scents and it is also a more mature perfume compared to her others. If you don't mind powdery perfumes moonlight is very nice and it's also probably one of her most underrated perfumes. 2017
Cloud Cloud is probably her most well known perfume. Although it is marketed as a woman's perfume it can be unisex. It's a very clean scent, sweet, lactonic. It's supposed to smell like lavender, pear, bergamot, whipped cream, coconut and vanilla orchid. Personally I don't really like the way it smells out of the bottle but it might smell better on. 2018
Thank you, next Thank you, next is a sweet coconut vanilla fragrance. It's supposed to smell like raspberry, pear, coconut, pink rose and macarons. It has very mixed reviews, some people love it and some people for some reason think that it smells like dill pickles. Personally i hated this scent when i smelled it. It was sour at first and then just sickening. But the notes sound very nice so I think it depends on the person because it is still quite popular. 2019
R.E.M REM is a sweet, powdery, fruity, lavender fragrance. It smells like zefir, caramel, salt, fig, quina, lavender and pear blossom. It has very good reviews. It is different than her other perfumes and it's very nice. 2020
Thank you, next 2.0 Thank you, next 2.0 is fruity, sweet and woody. It's supposed to smell like Apple juice, strawberry, pomegranate, white orchid, jasmine and marshmellow. Based on reviews it's a better verison of the original Thank you, next, it doesn't have a sour opening and it's more sweet and refreshing. 2021
God is a woman God is a woman is a fruity, sweet, vanilla fragrance. It smells like sweet pear, vanilla, ambrette, turkish rose and sandalwood. It's definetly one of my favourite perfumes ever. It smells so good and it lasts for at least 3-4 hours. 2021
Cloud intense Cloud intense is a sweet, amber, woody, powdery fragrance. It's supposed to smell like lavender, bergamot, pear, whipped cream, praline, coconut, and vanilla orchid. This perfume has good reviews, many people prefer this version to the original cloud, however cloud is a more generic and clean fragrance. 2021
