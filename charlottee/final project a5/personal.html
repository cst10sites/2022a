Her personal life

Ariana Grande has dated many famous celebrities including her most well known and recent boyfriends were Mac Miller who was an actor and singer-songwriter and Pete Davidson, an actor and comedian. Recently she married a real estate agent in LA named Dalton Gomez. They met when she was looking to buy a house in Los Angeles and Dalton was showing her listings. They started dating in early 2020, but they didn't announce their relationship until may 2020. They got engaged in December 2020 and then they got married in May 2021. Ariana Grande has a very close relationship with her family. She is especially close to her grandmother on her mother's side and her older half-brother Frankie Grande. He is always there to support her. As for her friends she is quite close to many people that she has worked with in the past for example Elizabeth Gillies, who acted as Jade in Victorious. One of her childhood best friends that she is still very close to is Alexia Luria, an american food blogger.
