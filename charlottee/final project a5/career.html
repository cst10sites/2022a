Her career

Ariana Grande was born on june 26, 1993 in Boca Raton, Florida. She started singing at a very young age in plays and at public events. her first "big moment" was being in the broadway musicals, 'Beauty and the Beast' and 'The Wizard of Oz'. After acting in many ensemble tv shows mostly from nikilodeon, she decided to focus more on her music career. She came out with her debut album called 'Yours Truly' which was immediately a big success. It broke multiple records and was at the top of the 'Billbord top 100' chart for multiple weeks. Hundreds of thousands of copies of her album were sold. She continued to release more music and she just continued to gain more and more popularity throughout the years. She is now one of the top singers in the world. The reasons that she became so popular so quickly is because of her songwriting and amazing vocal skills.
