Who is Ariana Grande?

Ariana Grande is a 28 year-old singer-songwriter and actor from the united states. She is a very successful actor as she has been in many shows and movies including lead roles in Victorious and Sam and Cat. Of course she is also a very successful singer, she is the 6th most popular artist in the world on spotify and her top song, thank you next has over 1.6 billion streams. The genres that her songs are, is usually pop/R&B, but when collaborating with other artists there are many different styles of music she has done.
