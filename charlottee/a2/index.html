My recipe: Chocolate chip cookies

By: Charlotte Eden

Introduction

This quick and easy delicious chocolate chip cookie recipe should only take about 30 minutes to make, and it barely uses any ingredients. You don't need to be experienced to make these cookies and they will keep fresh for multiple days. This recipe should make about 24 cookies depending on the size you make them.

This is one of my favourite recipies/things to make because it is very simple and easy to make, it barely takes any time and it is very delicious. I also have all of the ingredients almost always at my house.

Ingredients and Measurements:

  • 1½ cup flour
  • ½ granulated sugar
  • ⅓ cup of brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt(sea salt or household)
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • ¾ cup of butter (one stick)
  • ¾ cup of semi-sweet chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F
  2. In a medium bowl, mix together the flour, sugar, salt and baking powder until its evenly mixed up.
  3. Add in the softened butter and the vanilla extract and mix it in.
  4. Mix in the egg until the dough looks even.
  5. Gently fold the chocolate chips into the mixture.
  6. On a parchment paper lined baking sheet, place about a tablespoon worth of dough for each cookie about 2 inches apart.
  7. Place the cookies in the oven and set the timer for 7 minutes.
  8. After seven minutes turn and check the cookies and put them back in the oven for another 5 minutes.
  9. Check to see if they are done, ( they should be cooked on the sides and a little bit gooey on the inside) if they are not done but them back in until they are done.
  10. Take the cookies out of the oven after the timer and leave them to cool for a few minutes.
