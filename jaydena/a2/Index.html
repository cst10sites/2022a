Creme Brulée



This is a recipe on how to make Creme Brulée. This is my favorite desert to make because it is so easy
and so good. I chose this recipe because it takes almost no effort and time (apart from the cooling of the custards). It is extremely
simple yet extremely effective at making the people you serve this dish to think you're a better chef than you actually are.

Ingredients

  • approx 2 cups heavy cream
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 Tbsp brown or white sugar
  • ½ - 1 Tbsp white sugar

Instructions

  1. preheat oven to 300°F
  2. In a cup with a volume of 2 cups or more, pour sugar
  3. Crack eggs and separate yolks, put eggs in cup
  4. pour in the tsp of vanilla extract
  5. Fill cup with cream until it reaches two cups of total liquid, put excess cream back
  6. Whisk with fork until egg globules and sugar crystals have dissolved
  7. Pour custard into ramekins and place ramekins in a water-filled tray (DO NOT LET WATER SPILL INTO RAMEKINS)
  8. Place in preheated oven for 30 minutes
  9. Remove from oven, cover, and cool for an hour
  10. When custards are cool, uncover and sprinkle with sugar
  11. Blast sugar with butane torch until the sugar has caramelized. Let cool until caramel hardens
  12. Serve with fruit, berries or whipped cream and enjoy!
