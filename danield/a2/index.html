My Recipe: Crepes

Daniel D.

Introduction

The recipe that I'll be sharing will be Homemade Crepes. In my free time whenever I favour a tasty breakfast, I may decide to make some for my family. It contains a variety of ingredients, with a mix of fruits and sweets. I chose to share this recipe because although it's very easy to make, it still has a great outcome, giving you a boost towards your day.

Ingredients

What you will need:

  • 1 ¾ cup of Pancake Mix
  • ½ cup of Water
  • ½ cup of Milk
  • At least ⅛ cup of Butter (depending on how many crepes you are making)
  • 1 Egg
  • Container of Nutella Hazelnut Spread
  • At least 1 Banana (depending on how many crepes you are making)
  • At least 3-5 Strawberries (depending on how many crepes you are making)
  • Whipped Cream

Instructions

  1. Grab a mixing bowl and pour in the Pancake Mix, Water and Milk.
  2. Crack the egg open and pour in the mixing bowl. Dispose of the shell. Mix the pancake mix with a whisk.
  3. Preheat a flat pan.
  4. Place the portion of Butter onto it, rotating the pan to spread the butter around as it melts.
  5. Pour a portion of the Pancake Mix onto the pan.
  6. Wait until the up side seems to turn to a faintly darker-golden colour. If so, check if the down side is cooked. If so, flip the pancake using a flip.
  7. Once the other side has done cooking, transfer the plain crepe over to a plate.
  8. Using a butter knife, dip into a Nutella Hazelnut Spread container and spread out along the middle section of the crepe.
  9. Cut your strawberries and banana(s) using a butter knife and place them onto the nutella spread on the crepe.
  10. Fold up the two sides of the crepe across the middle and on top with each other.
  11. Squirt some whipped cream over the middle, and enjoy.
(Repeat steps 4-11 for when you make another crepe, you will have enough mix to make more)
