General Info

Translink is Metro Vancouver's Public Transit System. The company was formed in 1999 as the revolution from BC Transit's Vancouver Regional Transit System, which today operates the rest of BC (Victoria, Okanogan, etc). Today, their service is growing higher than ever before to meet the demands of Metro Vancouverites, in helping to get them from point A to point B while working hard to bring you the best service possible.

Annually, the system has offered customers to take a total of 2.7 Million trips. To ensure overall service across the entire region, Translink has service running in all towns/municipalities in the Metro Vancouver Area, the Lower Mainland, and certain parts of the Fraser Valley.
