Baked Salmon w Grapefruit Salad

Serves: 2; Prep Time: 15 minutes; Cook Time: 15 minutes; Calories; 354

Tools you'll need -

  • sheet pan
  • parchment paper
  • fish spatula

Ingredients -

  • ¾lb fresh salmon filet
  • one avocado, cubed
  • one grapefruit, peeled, segmented
  • four cups of loosely packed marche (or any other kind of spring greens)
  • one lemon
  • honey mustard vinaigrette
  • salt and pepper

Method

  • Preheat oven to 350, place salmon on a parchment lined baking sheet season it with salt and pepper, then bake for 15 minutes or until opaque and flakes easily with a fork.
  • While the salmon is baking, use a paring knife to peel the grapefruit, cutting away skin and slicing each wedge away from the inner skin
  • Assemble the salad on each plate by layering greens, avocado and grapefruit wedges and dress lightly with the vinaigrette, then divide the salmon into two pieces and dress with lemon juice. Finally, plate and serve the salad
