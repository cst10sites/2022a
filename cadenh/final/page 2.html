Over the course of his career, Portuguese footballer Cristiano Ronaldo has received five Ballon d'Or/FIFA Ballon d'Or awards, the most for a European player. Widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, Ronaldo holds the record for most goals and assists in the UEFA Champions League (140 and 42 respectively), and the record for most goals in the UEFA European Championship (14), its qualification stage (31), and the FIFA Club World Cup (7), as well as most goals scored in a UEFA Champions League season (17), most international goals (115), and most appearances in a European national team (180). He has scored a record 801 senior career goals for club and country. Moreover, he is one of the few recorded players to have made over 1,100 professional career appearances.

Goals and Scoring

  • Ronaldo is Real Madrid's greatest ever goalscorer, finding the back of the net 450 times for Los Blancos.
  • Ronaldo is the top scorer in the history of the Champions League with 136 goals, most of them coming with Real Madrid.
  • Ronaldo holds the record for the most goals scored for Real Madrid in a single season with 61.
  • Ronaldo has scored more hat-tricks than any other Real Madrid player with 48.
  • Ronaldo is the only Man Utd player to win the FIFA Puskas Award (award for best goal during the year)
  • Ronaldo is the fastest player ever to score 100 goals for Juventus
  • Ronaldo holds the record for the longest consecutive run of matches scoring for Juventus in Serie A with 11 in a row.
  • Ronaldo has scored the most goals in the history of international football - and naturally the Portugal national team, too - with 115. He overtook Iran's Ali Daei in September 2021.
  • Ronaldo is the first and only player ever to score 10 international hat-tricks.
