Introduction

We will be making a assoser filet mignon, this will be served with a side of mashed potatoes and a light salad. This meal should take around 40-50 minutes to complete. This is my favourite meal as it is very flavorful and is very simple to make.

What the food should end up looking like

Ingredients

  • Fillet mignon
  • Two cloves of garlic
  • Half a stick of butter
  • Potatoes of your choice
  • Asparagus
  • Milk
  • Thyme
  • Salt and pepper

    • Moreover you will need a skillet, a pot to boil the potaotes and a potato masher.

    Instructions

    1. Season your steak on all sides evenly with salt and peper, you need to try and get the sides aswell as the top and bottom
    2. Skin and cut your potatoes in half twice
    3. Put them into boiling water and add 3/4 of teaspoon of salt, then bring it down to medium-low heat
    4. The poatoes should take around 15 minutes, now put your skillet on high heat preparing it for the steak
    5. Once the potatoes are halfway done, put the steak on to the skillet while it is on high heat
    6. flip the steak after searing it for a minute and a half, and sear the other side for another minute and a half
    7. The potatoes should be done now, drain them and return them to the same pot on low heat to evaporate any excess moustiure
    8. Now turn the skillet down to low-medium heat and add the butter, garlic and thyme and continously baste the steak flipping every 2 minutes for a total of 7-8 minutes including the time it took to sear the steak
    9. Now mash your potatoes until there are no lumps add butter,pepper and 1/4 teaspoon of salt continously mashing and adding milk untill it reach your desired consistantcy
    10. Your steak should now be done, take it off the skillet and place it on to your plate with the thyme on top from the skillet, and let it rest for 6 minutes
    11. Now add your mash potatoes and asperagus
    12. Relax and enjoy your meal!
