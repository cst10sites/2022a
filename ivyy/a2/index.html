Souffle Pancake

Souffle pancake is a pancake made using souffle techniques as its name. Egg whites are whipped with sugar into a glossy think meringue then mixed with a batter made with the yolks. The meringue is a key to making souffle pancake much more fluffy, jiggle, sweet, and soft compared to the original pancake. It tastes like airy vanilla-flavoured pancakes that have a light sweetness to them. When people want to enjoy something new only with the same ingredient for a pancake, souffle pancake is the best choice. Maple syrup is one of the most popular toppings for souffle pancakes among various delicious toppings. For me, I love to eat souffle pancakes with fluffy whipped cream and fresh strawberries.


Ingredients

These are required ingredients to make a souffle pancake.

  • hand mixer
  • heavy whipped cream ½ (120g)
  • granulated sugar 2½ Tbsp (20g)
  • 2 large eggs
  • whole milk ½ Tbsp (22g)
  • vanilla extract ¼ tsp
  • cake flour ¼ cup (30-34g)
  • baking powder ½ tsp (2g)
  • vegitable oil 1 Tbsp
  • paper towel
  • water 1 Tbsp (for streaming)

Recipe

  1. Place whipped cream and 1½ granulated sugar in the medium bowl. Beat 4-5 min or until stiff peaks with a hand mixer. Refrigerate whipped cream.
  2. Seperate egg yolks and egg whites. Place egg whites in the freezer.
  3. Pour the whole milk and vanilla extract in egg yolks and whisk until thick and frothy. Sift the cake flour and baking powder. Whisk to combine thoroughly but do not over-mix.
  4. Heat frying pan to 150° over the lowest heat.
  5. Take egg whites out from the freezer. Beat the egg whites with 2 Tbsp(25g) of granulated sugar to form the meringue.
  6. Take ½ of egg whites and add into the egg yolk mixture and gently fold in without breaking the air bubble in the egg white. Add ½ of remaining egg whites into the mix. Fold it again.
  7. Grease the frying pan with vegetable oil and remove visible oil with a paper towel.
  8. Start with two scoops for each pancake. Add the third scoop. Add water in the frying pan for streaming and put the lid on.
  9. Remove the cover after 2 min and add more knowledge to a pancake.
  10. Gently flip pancakes with a flat spatula after 4 min.
  11. Repeat the same step as steps 10 and 11.
  12. If the surface of pancakes turns into delicious soft brown, place them on the plate.
  13. Enjoy your souffle pancake with various sauces and fruits aside.
