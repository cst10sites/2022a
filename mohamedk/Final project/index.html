Introduction

Didier Yves Drogba Tébily (born 11 March 1978) is an Ivorian professional footballer who played as a striker. He retired November 8, 2018. He is the all-time top scorer and former captain of the Ivory Coast national team. He is best known for his career at Chelsea, for whom he has scored more goals than any other foreign player and is the club's fourth highest goal scorer of all time.
