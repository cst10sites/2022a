Spaghetti Cacio e Pepe

Today we will be making spaghetti cacio e pepe.spaghetti Cacio e pepe is a pasta dish from the cuisine of the city of Rome. It is a very delicious dish with lots of flavour.

Ingredients

  • 1lb dried spaghetti
  • ¼cup olive oil
  • 2tbsp butter
  • 4oz grated Pecorino Romano cheese
  • 2tsp fresh cracked black pepper

Instructions

  1. Start by filling a pot with water and letting it boil.
  2. Then add you pasta to the boilling water and the olive oil. Toss until pasta is coated
  3. Lower heat to medium and add butter and ¾ of cheese. Toss until pasta is coated. Add pepper and toss again.
  4. Remove from heat and taste.
  5. Serve with remaining cheese, more pepper and a little drizzle of olive oil if you're into that kind of thing.
