This is an example of a ordered list. These are the directions necessary to make KD

  1. Put six cups of heavily salted water on the boil (salt until the water is as salty as the sea)
  2. Once the water has come to a rolling boil (100°c.), turn the element down to a simmer and add the macaroni
  3. Stir the macaroni every few minutes
  4. Poor the cooked macoroni into a strainer once it is al dente
  5. Add the butter, the milk, the cheese packet and the cooked macaroni into a pot and stir until wet and saucy
  6. You can add pepper or ketchep (but not both) to the mac n'cheese at the very end
  7. Enjoy!!
