Introduction

In this recipe I will be teaching you how to make the most nutrious and delicious meal, KD (otherwise known as Kraft dinner.) There are many reasons for why this is my favorite meal. Firstly, it is one of the most delicious creations. It also is extremely cheap and very easy to find at really any store you can think of. Most importantly, it is so simple a child could make it, (whih is why I am able to make it.) To make the cooking process even easier you can get the stuff that comes ready to be micro-waved, but today you will learn how to make the gourmet kind, right out of the fancy box.

Ingredients

  • The macaroni and cheese powder included in the box
  • A few large pinches of salt
  • 6 cups of water
  • ¼ of butter
  • ¼ of milk
  • Pepper or ketchup,(if you are feeling crazy!!)

Instructions

  1. Put six cups of heavily salted water on the boil (salt until the water is as salty as the sea)
  2. Once the water has come to a rolling boil (100°c.), turn the element down to a simmer and add the macaroni
  3. Stir the macaroni every few minutes
  4. Poor the cooked macoroni into a strainer once it is al dente
  5. Add the butter, the milk, the cheese packet and the cooked macaroni into a pot and stir until wet and saucy
  6. You can add pepper or ketchep (but not both) to the mac n'cheese at the very end
  7. Enjoy!!
