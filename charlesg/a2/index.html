How to make a sandwich

In this short paragraph, I'll be teaching you how to make a delicious sandwich. Sandwich is so easy to make, and they taste so good. After you know how to make a sandwich, you can make it everyday and bring to school for lunch. The whole process is going to be about 5 minutes or so. Very Convienent. I really like snadwich because they always taste so good and easy.

Ingredients:

  • 2 pieces of bread
  • cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 pieces of ham

Steps

  1. cut ½ of the bread
  2. boil the egg in 100° C water and cut them in ½
  3. put cheese on bread
  4. put eggs on bread
  5. put the ham in between
