Pork Meatball Soup

My recipe is on pork meatball soup. It is a meal I had a lot growing up and I really enjoyed it. I would classify it as one of my comfort foods. The soup is really easy to make with minimal ingredients and not to many steps. There is also some things that you can add to the soup to give it some more flavour but that is up to you. This is also a recipe that my grandmther made for my mom when she was a kid and that is why it has importance in the family and to me.

Ingredients In the soup

  • Ground pork 200 grams
  • Pork broth, 1L
  • Kale, any amount
  • Rice,200 grams
  • Mushrooms, any amount

    • Step by step process

  • Bring pork broth to a boil at 100°C
  • Add kale and mushrooms to cook into broth
  • While broth cooks fry pork then add to broth
  • Let sit for 3 mins
  • Poor into bowl
  • Add rice to bowl then mix
