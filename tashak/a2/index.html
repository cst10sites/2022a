Simple Hot Chocolate Recipe

This recipes total cook and prep time is around 6 minutes and it serves up to four people.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of milk
  • ¼ cup of sugar
  • ¼ cup of cocoa powder
  • ½ cup of chocolate chips
  • ¼ teaspoon of vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Put your milk, cocoa powder and sugar in a pot or saucepan
  2. Put the heat on low/medium or let the milk hit about 70°C and whisk until warm
  3. Once your mixture is warm add in chocolate chips
  4. Keep mixing chocolate chips until they are evenly distributed into your mixture
  5. Stir in vanilla extract
  6. Add whipped cream or your topping of choice and serve while warm
  7. Enjoy!!
