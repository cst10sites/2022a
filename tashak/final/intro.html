Canada's Most Decorated Olympian

Who is Penny Oleksiak?

Penny Oleksiak is a 21 year old Olympic swimmer who has won a total of 7 Olympic medals spanning over 2 Olympics. In the summer of 2021 she beat Clara Hughes and Cindy Klassen by 1 medal to take the title of the most decorated Canadian Olympian. In her Olympic career she won 4 bronze, 2 silver, and even 1 gold medal. Including World Championships the athlete has won 33 medals. Although she only rose to fame after the 2016 Olympics, she has been turning heads since 2015 during the Junior World Championships. She competed and picked up 6 medals. The swimmer has set many records for Canada in Butterfly and Freestyle. Overall she is in the top 150 most decorated Olympic athletes of all time.


Penny Oleksiak has been swimming since she was 9 years old. Even though she had a bit of a late start, she was able to win her first Olympic medal 7 years later. At the age of 16 she became the first person born in the 2000s to win an Olympic medal for her sport. She also became the first person to win Canada a gold medal in the 2016 Rio Olympics.
