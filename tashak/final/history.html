Canada's Most Decorated Olympian

Records

Penny Oleksiak has broken almost every single Canadian record possible. The 21-year-old has become one of Canada's best athletes.


  1. Penny Oleksiak is Canada's most decorated Olympian
  2. She was the 1st person born in the 2000s to win an Olympic medal from swimming
  3. She anchored the Canadian women's relay team to win their 1st ever Olympic medal
  4. In the 2016 Olympics she was the 1st Canadian to win a medal
  5. She holds the current Canadian record for the fastest 100 meter freestyle
  6. Holds the current Canadian record for the fastest 50 meter Butterfly
  7. Oleksiak holds the junior world record for 100 meter freestyle
  8. She is in the top 150 most decorated athletes of all time



History

Penny Oleksiak grew up in an extremely athletic family. Her brother, Jaime Oleksiak, is an NHL defenseman for the Seattle Kraken. Her sister, Hayley Oleksiak, was a NCAA rower and had multiple top-10 finishes. Penny Oleksiak learned how to swim at 9 years old in her neighbour's pool. A couple years after she joined the Toronto swim club and swam under Bill O'Toole. Just 3 years later she was already representing Canada at the Australian Age Group Championship. There she won 8 medals. Later in that year, the teenager went to the FINA World Championships where she won 6 medals. In the next year she went on the qualify for the 2016 Olympics where she won 4 medals. For the next couple of years Oleksiak swam in the Commonweatlh Games and 2 FINA World Championships where she picked up an abundance of medals. During the pandemic the athlete worked as hard as ever to qualify for the upcoming 2020 Olympics. She qualified and competed in 6 events where she won 3 more Olympic medals, making her the Canadian legend she is now. Oleksiak plans to make another comeback in the 2024 Paris Olympics.
