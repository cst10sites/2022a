Oleksiak won 3 medals by herself and the other 4 with the help of her teammates. In the 2016 Olympics she competed with Brittany MacLean, Katerine Savard, and Taylor Ruck. The four swimmers won 2 bronze medals, making them the first women's relay in Canada to ever win a medal. In the 2020 Olympics Oleksiak won 1 medal by herself and the other 2 with teamates. She was joined by Kayla Sanchez, Margaret MacNeil,and Rebecca Smith for the 4 x 100 freestyle relay and for the medley she was joined by Sydney Pickrem, Margaret MacNeil, and Kylie Masse. A medley relay is a relay where you compete all 4 different strokes: backstroke, butterfly, breaststroke, and freestyle.

Honourable Mentions

In the 2020 Tokyo games Penny Oleksiak came 4th in two events. She came just short of a medal in the 100 meter Freestyle swimming 52.59 seconds. The 3rd place swimmer had a time of 52.52 seconds. In the womens 4 x 200 meter Freestyle relay Canada placed 4th again with a time of 7:43.77 minutes. Finshing 2 seconds after third place.
