Spaghetti Carbonara Recipe

I am going to be making Spaghetti carbonara, it's an easy italian recipe.

Ingredients

  • Any dried spaghetti
  • 2 Eggs
  • ½ Grated parmesan
  • 4 bacon, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tablespoon of fresh parsley
  • Salt and blackpepper

Instructions

  1. In a pot with boiling salted water, add the pasta and boil it; reserve ½ cup water and drain
  2. In a bowl, mix the eggs parmesan together and set aside.
  3. Heat a skillet to 400°F cook for about 7 minutes.
  4. Stir in garlic until fragrant, about 1 minute
  5. stir in pasta and egg mixture, and toss around to combine; season with salt and pepper.
  6. Add reserved pasta water, 1tbsp at a time, until desired consistency is reached.
  7. Add in some parsley.
