About

Bloodhound, starting from a small village that was very against guns, used a gun to defeat a monster that was terrorizing the village. He was shunned, However the monster didnt actually die, it came back, angry that Bloodhound had stolen one of its horns. It killed his uncle Arthur and then was finally actually killed by Bloodhound, with the gun. Arthurs dying breath was like "yo, actually those things arent too bad you can come back to us". But Bloodhound was like "thanks but like... no" and left anyways outfitting himself with more tech, but still keeping, and honering the ways of his ancestors and his culture.

Abilities

Every Legend needs something to make itself special, sure they all have very different stories and personalities. But we need something gameplay wise. Not just hitboxes, not visual. Abilities. Every Legend has three different abilities you can use. Their Passive ability is something that can happen in the background constantly, like passive shield healing with wattson. Or it could be triggered when you do a specific action, like using another ability, or using something in the environment. The list goes on. Their next ability is called a Tactical Ability, after you use it, there is a countdown before you can use it again, sometimes you can use it multiple times before the countdown starts, the countdown could be long or short. You press a keybind, it performs the ability, then the countdown starts. Simple, easy, convenient. The final ability is called The Ultimate Ability but most players just call it an "Ult". The Ultimate is used for when you need something bigger, sometimes it can effect other teammates sometimes it can effect what you can see, sometimes it directly hurts or affects enemies. It all depends on the Legend being used. Every set of abilities is different. So lets get on with the technological tracker, the one and only Bloodhound!

Passive

Tracker, you can see actions done by enemies nearby if they were done in the last 90 seconds, and you can see how long ago they are, if you've played bloodhound before and know the techniques, you can accurately track the movements and what happened in a battle ground that was just vacated. It's very interesting and gives you a lot of knowledge about what had happened there.

Tactical

Scan, i dont know the actual name, but everyone just calls it "Scan", its probably "eye of the allfather" but thats too long to say, so we dont. Scan shoots a wall of yellow light that highlights all enemies in range, and tells you where they are. It highlights them for a few seconds. It's helpful for scouting out a building easily, and seeing if anyone is hiding from you.

Ultimate

Beast Of The Hunt (Furry Mode) is a great ultimate, increasing base stats, making it easier to track people, decreasing recharge time of Tactical significantly. All around very good ultimate. 10/10

Playstyle

Aggressive

Oh is it fun to play Bloodhound aggressively. Using scans to find enemies hiding from you, your tracker makes it almost impossible to escape. Your ultimate makes you the fastest character in the game. There is no escape from a good aggressive bloodhound.

Defensive

Bloodhound is also very good defensively, Using scans to cover your retreat, your ultimate to scare them off and keep them at bay, or even turn the tables. Could also be used to run away in panic, but like... Your choice. Unfortunately the only thing you can use your tracker for defensively is by seeing if anybody is ahead of you that you could possibly run into, but that isn't fully reliable because they could come from the side.. but whatever..

furry mode

Its a common joke in the playerbase that Bloodhound's Ultimate ability is called "beast of the hunt" so people call it "furry mode" and it's great because I am also a furry. Basically this is all about how to use the ultimate ability. Here. We. GO. Use it, you get speed, and a really fast recharge on your tactical ability. you get double speed, your vision goes black and white so there isnt any sensory disturbance in your eyes, the envoronment gets quiter, and you make a growling noise thing, as well as a roar type thing when you enter furry mode. Also your eyes glow red, and the roar is very loud (intimidation factor) because people are scared of furries.

How I Play

I play bloodhound in a very conservative way, since i die very early in the game most games, i never really have a chance to use my Ult. Therefore i've gotten used to playing without it, that's another reason i die alot, i dont use abilities as much as I should. And that can be problematic for my survival. I tend to use scans randomly, when im bored, it usually alerts enemies to our presence which is good if you want a fight and are confident, but if not.. well you're probably gonna die.
