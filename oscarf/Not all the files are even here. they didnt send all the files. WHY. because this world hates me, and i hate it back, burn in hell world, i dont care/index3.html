About

Octane is speed. That is literally all you need to know. SPEED. He blew his legs of for more speed, he gave himself a crippling adrenaline addiction for SPEED, he replaced his exploded legs with robot ones for SPEED, he basically became drugs for SPEED.

Abilities

Every Legend needs something to make itself special, sure they all have very different stories and personalities. But we need something gameplay wise. Not just hitboxes, not visual. Abilities. Every Legend has three different abilities you can use. Their Passive ability is something that can happen in the background constantly, like passive shield healing with wattson. Or it could be triggered when you do a specific action, like using another ability, or using something in the environment. The list goes on. Their next ability is called a Tactical Ability, after you use it, there is a countdown before you can use it again, sometimes you can use it multiple times before the countdown starts, the countdown could be long or short. You press a keybind, it performs the ability, then the countdown starts. Simple, easy, convenient. The final ability is called The Ultimate Ability but most players just call it an "Ult". The Ultimate is used for when you need something bigger, sometimes it can effect other teammates sometimes it can effect what you can see, sometimes it directly hurts or affects enemies. It all depends on the Legend being used. Every set of abilities is different. So lets get on with the speed demon himself, Octane!

Passive

Octane is all about speed, he doesn't want to wast time on healing, so he regenerates 2 health every half second as his passive. This is also super helpful because his Tactical, his Stim shot does about 20 damage to himself.

Tactical

The Stim shot, giving you 50% more movement speed for a span of 5 seconds. However this stim shot also deals 20 damage to yourself, however it is unable to kill you so you don't need to worry about that. The fact that it recharges before it runs out means that right when the stim ends, you can stab yourself again and BOOM! More speed. Stimming repeatedly however isn't very smart, because it just keeps your health at 1, so the only thing between you and death are your shields, which can be broken with relative ease by a skilled player, so watch out for that, and keep a lot of healing on hand just in case.

Ultimate

It's a jump pad. It recharges so incredibly quickly, taking maybe 30 seconds to recharge after using it. You can have 5 out at once before the next one destroys the oldest one. You can however just shoot the thing enough and it will be destroyed. Just running into the jump pad will get you more height and less distance, so its good for scaling walls, and sliding into the jump pade will do the opposite, more distance, less height, better for movement. There is another way to do it, and that is stop right before the jump pad, let go of all movement keys and then punch, you will lunge forward slightly with the punch and then be sent straight up. landing on the jump pad again without having moved causes you to bounce straight up again. however moving too much will bounce you regularly, in the direction you were moving.

Playstyle

Being a character based entirely on speed and movement, it is necessary to have serious momentum in every attack, even when defending you need to be moving, octane does not sit still. He blew his legs off to get a speed record in a deadly race. He's an adrenaline junkie. He's Octane, speed is the name of his game.

Aggressive

Playing aggressively with Octane fills your brain with dopamine if you do it right, sprinting in with a stim and launching yourself into the air with a jump pad, landing in the middle of enemies guns blazing, dodging everything. It paints a picture of mad bravery and a lack of self-preservation. That is NOT HOW TO PLAY OCTANE AGGRESSIVELY! You must stay with your team, use the aggressiveness to scout out areas and to get back into the fight after taking some time to heal. Be the right amount of aggressive, you must be more aggressive than your team (because that's just octane) but you shouldn't go over aggressive because that will get you gunned down by three different people, then your team will have a disadvantage, and thats not good.

Defensive

It's very difficult to play Octane in a defensive fashion, Octane is an attacking class character, the best I think you could play defensive is by running, to be completely fair thats the best way to do anything with Octane: RUN, and FAST, because octane is the definition of movement, if you play octane you need to be ready for Hight Octane gameplay (puns :D).

Creative

Having a jump pad can be used to be creative, if you look up some creative spots, you can put your jump pad to get behind enemies, or rotate incredibly quickly There is one spot on Olympus, where you can launch yourself, and use the double jump to skip 3 minutes of rotation by going UNDER the map, its so insane. If you've gotten bored of Octane try looking up some creative jump pad placements, the double jump comes in incredibly helpful so many times.

How I Play

I love movement. Octane is one of the best characters for pure speed, the ability to run fast confuses player's tracking, if you change speeds dramatically and randomly, they have a harder time tracking your movement. also being able to run away is awesome, although to be fair, i usually just use octane's stim to collect loot, and do some sneaky stuff (i once managed to sneak in and get two banners and run away without being spotted). It's very satisfying to pull something off. I don't really use my Jump Pad for battle and running much, more for repositioning, because people on the ground have high accuracy, people in the air from a jump pad have significantly more bullet spread, so if you're in the air, you cant hit people, but people on the ground can hit you.
