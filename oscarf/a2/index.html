Assignment 2:My Recipe

What We Will Be Making

Today I will show you all the recipe for Tandoori Chicken, a very tasty meal with a really good spice mix, that can serve about 5 people. I have fond memories of this recipe as it is one of the first I ever made with someone else. The end product should have a dark(ish) orange look to it from the spices, its not incredibly spicy if you are worried about that. If you want you can serve this meal on top of rice, in fact I highly suggest you do that. So without further a do, lets get started!

Ingredients needed

  • 1 whole chicken cut into 10-12 pieces
  • 200 grams of dahi/curd
  • 1 tsp of turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp of red chili powder
  • 150 grams of ginger garlic paste

Ingredients for cooking

  • 1 tbsp of soft salted butter
  • 2 tbsp oil (for the pan)
  • 1 piece of charcoal (if you want more of a smokey flavor)

Garnish with

  • Coriander Leaves
  • Onion slices
  • Lemon Halves
  • add salt to taste

Method

  1. make deep vertical cuts into the chicken (to let the spice mix get everywhere)
  2. for marination add yogurt, red chili powder, ginger garlic paste, and salt to taste
  3. mix marination together thoroughly
  4. place chicken pieces in marination and make sure to get the marination in every nook and cranny in the chicken
  5. keep aside marinated chicken (still in marination) for ½ an hour
  6. if wanted, start making rice after marination timer is done
  7. in a pan, add oil and salted butter, heat said pan to about 500°F
  8. cook each side of the chicken until golden brown (or the smaller edges are slightly crispy) each side on the heat for approx 10-12 minutes

For Extras and Serving

  1. Heat charcoal untill red in color
  2. in a bowl where the cooked chicken sits, put a small steel glass that will fit the charcoal deep in the bowl, add a bit of oil, and put a lid on the smoking bowl (this gives it a more smokey flavor)
  3. Garnish with Coriander Leaves, Onion, and Lemon
  4. SERVE AND ENJOY!
